Sources and font notices

The historical source is George Thibaut, The Śulvasūtras, Calcutta, C. B. Lewis, Baptist Mission Press, 1875, reprinted from the Journal of the Asiatic Society of Bengal, part I. The app includes complete printed pages 6–7, 12–15 and 26–28, rendered from PDF pages 12–13, 18–21 and 32–34 of the Oxford copy digitized by Google.

Original volume and scan record. The historical Sanskrit, English text and page images are public domain. The Internet Archive record marks the volume NOT_IN_COPYRIGHT. The images are complete page renderings, not reconstructions from OCR. New diagrams, English translations and notes are MIT, Anton Soloviev, 2026.

The Sanskrit spelling, spaces and punctuation are normalized from the print. Thibaut’s translation, commentarial interpretations, and his proposed historical derivations remain distinguishable. His social and historical judgments are part of the source document, not findings adopted by this exhibit.

The repository’s THIRD_PARTY.md and docs/source-identities.json give source URLs and exact file identities. The complete volume and external scholarship require a network connection; bundled pages and these notices are part of the offline edition.
